Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Our approach

StreamGarden is built to collect as little as possible. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads or run trackers.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account, your email address and (optionally) a display name, so your Premium can follow you across devices.
  • Device check-in — a device identifier, app version and platform, used to deliver updates and to protect the service from abuse.
  • Payment — when you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Razorpay. We receive only a confirmation and a payment reference; we never receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.

The links you paste and the videos you download are processed to fetch the media and are not stored on our servers or linked to your identity.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your Premium active.
  • To deliver app updates and important notices.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

4. Third parties we use

  • Supabase — stores your account and check-in data securely.
  • Razorpay — processes Premium payments.
  • Public media resolvers used only to fetch the media you request.

These providers process data only to deliver their part of the service.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep account data only while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time — see the Contact page. We will delete it within a reasonable period unless we are required to keep it by law.

6. Children

StreamGarden is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

8. Contact

For any privacy request, see our Contact page.
