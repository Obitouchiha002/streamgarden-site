Download & install

Pick your device. Three quick steps.

No downloader is allowed in the app stores, so each system shows a one-time safety prompt. Here's exactly what you'll see — and the one tap past it.

Android

APK · 8 MB · 6.0+
  1. Tap Download. Chrome may warn about the file — that shows for every APK. Choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it. If installs are blocked, tap Settings, allow this source, go back.
  3. Tap Install → Open. Done.
Download APK · v2.7

Windows

Setup .exe · 10 / 11
  1. Download and run StreamGarden-Setup.exe.
  2. “Windows protected your PC” appears — it isn't paid-signed. Click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Finish installing. yt-dlp & ffmpeg are already inside.
Download for Windows · v1.5

macOS

Silicon & Intel · 10.15+
  1. Pick your chip (Apple menu → About This Mac), open the .dmg, drag to Applications.
  2. Open once — macOS says it “could not verify”. Click Done.
  3. Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway → Open.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it safe? What about those warnings?

Yes. The full source is public and it asks for no camera, microphone, location or contacts. The prompts — Chrome's APK notice, “Windows protected your PC”, macOS's “could not verify” — appear only because the app isn't signed with a paid store certificate, not because anything is wrong. Every open-source downloader ships this way.

Why isn't it on the app stores?

Google and Apple don't allow apps that download from platforms like YouTube. That's why NewPipe, Seal and every similar app are distributed outside the stores — exactly like this.

Which Mac build do I pick?

Apple menu → About This Mac. “Apple M1/M2/M3…” → choose Apple Silicon; “Intel” → choose Intel. Same app either way.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It's a single ₹99 payment, unlocked for life on your device, including all future updates. No renewals, no recurring charge.

How do I actually get Premium?

Message us on WhatsApp (button above), pay ₹99 by UPI, send the screenshot. We flip your device to Premium instantly. No card details ever leave your hands.

Does it collect my data?

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. The app registers a device identifier so updates and Premium can be applied — nothing about you, your files or your activity is collected.

Where do my downloads go?

Into a StreamGarden folder in your normal Downloads / storage — ordinary files, visible to every gallery, file manager and music player.
